The diseases people most fear in old age — heart attack and stroke, cancer, dementia, Parkinson's,
the slow loss of muscle and bone — look like separate problems needing separate answers. The
strongest single finding of the modifiable-risk literature is that they mostly are not. A **handful of
big levers** — not smoking, not carrying excess visceral fat, staying physically active, keeping blood
pressure, glucose and LDL in range, an unremarkable whole-food diet pattern, adequate sleep, little or
no alcohol, and (with age) protecting muscle, bone and balance — move risk across *several* of these
diseases at once. This is the **common-soil** picture: one wear-and-tear engine feeding many endpoints,
appraised in [[Big Rocks (Median)]] and [[Big Rocks (Elderly)]].

## What this page is — and is not

Because those big levers are shared, they are appraised in one place: [[Big Rocks (Median)]] (for a
drifting middle-aged adult) and [[Big Rocks (Elderly)]] (for an older one). **This page does not repeat
them.** It covers the opposite: the **disease-specific increments** — the smaller, per-disease levers
the shared big rocks miss. For each disease it gives a one-line map to the big rocks that matter most,
then only what is genuinely additional. The organizing claim is that these increments are **few and
mostly modest**: most of the achievable gain against ageing sits in the shared big rocks, and the
disease-specific tail is exactly that — a tail. Reporting how thin that tail is, is itself the result
-> [[Layer 1 - Ranking Interventions for a Stratum]].

Two framing rules carry through. **Population attributable fractions are not per-person effects** — a
"45% of dementia is attributable to 14 factors" figure is what *theoretically* would not occur if a
whole population's exposure vanished, not a share of any one person's risk. And the **loop is open**:
every grade here is a causal *judgement* on observational cohorts, never a lever checked against a
realized outcome. A clean read is not a validated promise.

## Cardiovascular disease — the pure common-soil case

**The big rocks that matter most: blood pressure, LDL/apoB, not smoking, visceral adiposity, physical
activity, and the diet pattern -> [[Big Rocks (Median)]] / [[Big Rocks (Elderly)]].** apoB is the
cholesterol-carrying-particle count, a refinement of LDL-C; Mendelian randomization is a natural-
experiment design using inherited gene variants as a lifelong randomiser.

CVD is the disease for which the shared-lever model is *most* complete: the big rocks essentially **are**
the cardiovascular risk factors. There is no meaningful non-big-lever increment to add here — which is
the point of leading with it. The one cross-disease note worth carrying is that pulling these levers
buys more than CVD: the same blood-pressure, LDL, adiposity, smoking and activity levers also lower
dementia risk (below), so their cross-disease breadth strengthens their ranking rather than competing
with it [[Dementia Prevention and Modifiable Risk Factors]].

## Cancer — a few genuine per-site increments beyond the big rocks

**The big rocks that matter most: not smoking (tobacco dwarfs every diet lever), avoiding overweight/
obesity, physical activity, and little or no alcohol -> [[Big Rocks (Median)]] / [[Big Rocks (Elderly)]].**
By breadth of sites and strength of evidence, the leading *diet-adjacent* cancer exposures after tobacco
are body fatness, alcohol and physical activity [@wcrf2018ter, Introduction] — all of them big rocks. What follows is only the residue those miss.

### Alcohol as a carcinogen — the no-safe-threshold twist

The alcohol lever lives in [[Big Rocks (Median)]], where the *mortality* harm begins at a sex-specific
**dose** — from \~25 g/day in women and \~45 g/day in men [@zhao2023, Results]. The cancer-specific increment is that on the **cancer** axis there
is *no* lower threshold: «there is no level of consumption below which there is no increase in the risk
of at least some cancers» [@wcrf2018ter, §5.1 Limit alcohol], and IARC classes alcoholic drinks a **Group 1** (established) human carcinogen.

Alcohol is a convincing/probable cause of cancers of the mouth/pharynx/larynx, oesophagus, breast,
colorectum, liver and stomach. The site numbers in the evidence are the *edge of the data*, not safe
levels — breast has
no identified threshold; the colorectal judgement rests on intakes above \~30 g/day, liver/stomach above
\~45 g/day [@wcrf2018ter, matrix asset p3, FN36-38]. Fuller
appraisal: [[Alcohol and Mortality and Vascular Disease]].

### Processed and red meat — colorectal, small in absolute terms

WCRF grades **processed meat a convincing** cause and **red meat a probable** cause of colorectal
cancer: per-dose associations of RR **1.16 (1.08-1.26) per 50 g/day** (processed) and RR **1.12
(1.00-1.25) per 100 g/day** (red, its lower bound touching the null) [@wcrf2018, 5.1.1.1 / 5.2.1.1]. WCRF's target is **no more than \~350 to
500 g cooked weight of red meat per week** and «very little, if any, processed meat» [@wcrf2018ter, §5.1 Limit red and processed meat].

In absolute terms the lever is small: for a realistic 3-servings/week cut, roughly **7 fewer cancer deaths per 1000 over
a lifetime** (low certainty) [@nutrirecs2019, Table 1]. Whether that warrants a population recommendation is a live cross-body disagreement, kept
on [[Should Adults Reduce Red and Processed Meat]]; the point here is only that meat is a genuine
colorectal-specific increment, and a modest one.

### The supplement trap — beta-carotene in smokers

The one *supplement* WCRF grades a **convincing cause** of cancer is high-dose beta-carotene, which
raises **lung cancer** risk in current and former smokers [@wcrf2018ter, §5.1]. It is the standing counter-example to "an antioxidant pill can't hurt":
supplementing an isolated nutrient at high dose is a different exposure from eating the food, and here
it does measurable harm in the exposed stratum -> [[Dietary Supplements Big Lever or Distraction]].

### The young-adulthood paradox — noted, not banked

Body fatness in *young adulthood* is associated with **lower** later breast-cancer risk — a probable-
graded arm running opposite to adult adiposity [@wcrf2018ter, §6.3]. Treat it as a unit-of-analysis warning (the label "body fatness" hides two timing-
dependent quantities), not a lever: it is an opposite-direction association with no stated mechanism and
obvious confounders, exactly the profile of a protective-arm artifact
-> [[The U-Shaped Association Artifact]].

### What is NOT held — the non-diet cancer levers

Large cancer-specific levers sit **outside** this wiki's diet-and-activity evidence base and are named
gaps, not findings: **UV / sun exposure** (the dominant modifiable skin-cancer lever), and the
**infectious** causes — HPV vaccination (cervical and oropharyngeal), hepatitis B/C, and *H. pylori*
eradication (stomach). None is held; the WCRF corpus behind
[[Diet Physical Activity and Cancer Prevention]] is diet/nutrition/activity by construction, so its
silence on them is a coverage limit, not evidence they are unimportant.

## Dementia and Alzheimer's — the richest set of true increments

**The big rocks that matter most: hypertension, diabetes, obesity, LDL, not smoking, physical activity
and avoiding excess alcohol — mostly in midlife -> [[Big Rocks (Median)]] / [[Big Rocks (Elderly)]].**
Seven of the 2024 Lancet Commission's 14 modifiable dementia factors *are* those cardiometabolic big
rocks, so pulling them buys cognition as a **second** patient-important outcome on top of CVD
[@livingston2024]. The Commission estimates the 14 factors
carry a population attributable fraction of **45.3%** — a modelled, communality-discounted, population
figure, not a per-person guarantee [@livingston2024]. What is
genuinely *dementia-specific*, beyond the shared rocks, is the set below.

### Hearing correction — the largest single dementia-specific lever

Hearing loss raises dementia risk (new meta-analysis **HR 1.37, 1.00-1.87**), with a dose-response by
severity, and it is *correctable* [@livingston2024]. Observed
hearing-aid users have **17% lower dementia** (0.77-0.90) and **19% lower cognitive-decline** (0.76-0.87)
risk than the uncorrected [@livingston2024]. The one RCT
(ACHIEVE) was **null overall** on 3-year cognition (difference -0.002) but showed a pre-specified **48%
protective effect in a high-risk subgroup** [@livingston2024].
So the causal case rests mainly on consistent observational data plus one subgroup, held at medium
confidence — absolute benefit is largest where baseline dementia risk is highest, and the barrier is
uptake, not availability -> [[Hearing Loss and Dementia]].

### Vision, cognitive and social engagement, head-injury protection, air quality

The remaining dementia-specific increments, each observational (relative risks for dementia incidence,
assumed causal by the Commission):

- **Correct uncorrected vision** (new 2024 factor) — RR **1.47 (1.36-1.60)**, and like hearing it is a
  fixable sensory input [@livingston2024].
- **Cognitive and social engagement** — cognitive stimulation at work carries **HR 0.79**; social
  isolation raises risk (**RR 1.57, 1.32-1.85**); depression is associated (**RR 2.25**, partly reverse-
  causal over the long prodrome) [@livingston2024].
- **Avoid traumatic brain injury** — **RR 1.66 (1.42-1.93)**; the lever is head protection (helmets,
  fall-avoidance) [@livingston2024].
- **Air pollution** — PM2.5 **HR 1.03 (1.02-1.05) per 1 µg/m³**, a late-life/lifelong environmental
  exposure [@livingston2024].

The operative timing rule is to lower these factors «early (the earlier, the better)» and keep them low
«throughout life (the longer, the better)», and risk is modifiable regardless of APOE genotype
[@livingston2024].

### The diet caveat — don't double-count it

A Mediterranean-diet pattern is associated with lower Alzheimer's risk (**RR 0.89, 0.84-0.93** per
adherence point) [@garciacasares2021], but this is
observational, healthy-user- and reverse-causation-prone, and its benefit is «indirectly» routed through
the cardiometabolic factors already counted as big rocks [@garciacasares2021]. So the diet pattern is a *route to pull the big rocks*, not a separate
dementia increment to add on top — it lives with the diet-pattern lever in [[Big Rocks (Median)]].

## Parkinson's disease — honestly thin

**The big rocks that matter most: physical activity — and it is the *only* credible modifiable lever
here, already a big rock -> [[Big Rocks (Median)]] / [[Big Rocks (Elderly)]].**

Parkinson's is the one disease in this set where the honest answer is **insufficient evidence**, not a
menu. In a 2021 umbrella review of 46 systematic reviews, **none rated high or moderate quality; 39
(\~85%) were critically low** under AMSTAR-2, and of 50 factors with significant associations only **19
survived to prospective evidence** [@chen2021pd]. Low-quality reviews do not launder into confidence by count. Reporting that ceiling — that
there is little here to optimise — is the decision-change.

The single most credible protective signal is **physical activity (RR 0.79, 0.68-0.91**, prospective,
dose-dependent by intensity) — but it is **already a big rock** for mortality, CVD and cognition, so it
buys a *possible* Parkinson's benefit as a rider, not a new lever [@chen2021pd]. The genuinely disease-specific residue is weak and mostly
low-certainty:

- **Pesticide / occupational exposure** — the most-discussed Parkinson's risk, yet it rests largely on
  case-control (bias-prone) data: pesticides OR **1.76 (1.56-2.04)**, with herbicides, solvents and
  farming also positive. Real but lower-certainty than its prominence implies (*attention is an
  anti-signal*) [@chen2021pd].
- **Coffee / tea / caffeine** — protective associations (caffeine RR **0.55, 0.43-0.71**; coffee 0.67)
  that are **reverse-causation-suspect**: Parkinson's has a years-to-decades prodrome that lowers
  consumption *before* diagnosis, which can manufacture a protective arm. Each must survive a lag or
  Mendelian-randomization check before it is believed [@chen2021pd].
- **Dairy / milk** — the one dietary *risk* signal (RR \~**1.40-1.56**), modest and of uncertain
  mechanism, not a demonstrated cause [@chen2021pd].

Smoking's famous *inverse* association (RR 0.64) is an **artifact to explain, never a lever** — likely
easier quitting in prodromal patients plus animal-only nicotine mechanism, and smoking's net harm buries
any signal -> [[Parkinsons Disease Modifiable Risk Factors]], [[The U-Shaped Association Artifact]].

## Frailty, sarcopenia and osteoporosis — no increment beyond the big rocks

**The big rocks that matter most: resistance (strength) training with adequate protein, balance and
functional exercise, and vitamin-D/calcium *only where deficient* -> [[Big Rocks (Elderly)]].**

These conditions are the ageing body's function-and-structure failures, and their levers are exactly the
function big rocks — appraised in full in [[Big Rocks (Elderly)]], including the finding that **balance/
functional exercise (not walking, not lifting alone) is the high-certainty fall-prevention lever**, and
that the vitamin-D/calcium pill is a **deficiency-repletion** lever, not a community-wide one. There is
**no disease-specific increment to add here** — which, like CVD, is the informative result: the levers
that preserve muscle, bone and balance are shared wear-and-tear levers, not a separate menu. This page
deliberately does not re-describe them.

## Healthspan versus length — a separate axis, and under-measured

Several of these levers act more on **function and trajectory** than on lifespan. Balance/functional
exercise prevents falls and fractures; hearing and vision correction preserve engagement; strength
training defends independence. The shape people most want — full function then a rapid end (compressed
morbidity) — is **preferred by many to a slow multi-decade decline of equal total length**, yet trials
count death and disease *events* far more than they measure the trajectory. Dementia is the clearest
case where the evidence speaks to shape: healthier lives push dementia onset back *further than* life
expectancy extends, compressing the years lived with it [@livingston2024].

Two disciplines follow. **Keep length and function as separate axes** — do not collapse them into one
score; the weighing of a good end against a long one is irreducibly the person's. And treat the quiet
mortality/incidence endpoints as **silent on trajectory**: where a lever's effect on the *shape* of
decline is unmeasured, that is a gap to name, not a zero to assume.

## What is NOT a disease-specific big lever

The anti-sprawl payoff: most of what fills longevity discourse ranks **low** against the shared big
rocks and adds little disease-specific increment. **Supplements** (beyond confirmed-deficiency
repletion) do not earn a place — the same nutrient that is protective in food can be inert or harmful as
a high-dose pill (beta-carotene above) -> [[Dietary Supplements Big Lever or Distraction]]. **Meal
timing, micronutrient fine-tuning and the specific-superfood hunt** are small-effect and heavily
published — literature volume runs *inverse* to effect size in a mature field. For someone who has the
big rocks handled, the honest message is that the remaining disease-specific levers are small and few,
which licenses stopping the optimisation rather than chasing it.

## Gaps — stated plainly, because they are the second product

- **Parkinson's is genuinely under-evidenced** -> [[Parkinsons Disease Modifiable Risk Factors]]. The
  field's own ceiling is low (39/46 reviews critically low); physical activity is the one credible lever
  and it is already a big rock. A Mendelian-randomization source would be needed to upgrade or kill the
  coffee/tea/activity protective arms; none is held.
- **Non-diet cancer levers are unheld** — UV/sun and the infectious causes (HPV, hepatitis, *H. pylori*)
  are large modifiable levers outside this wiki's diet/activity corpus, named here as gaps.
- **The aggregate cross-disease effect cannot be pooled.** Each lever's per-disease effect sits on a
  different outcome scale (fewer strokes vs fewer cancer deaths vs a dementia PAF vs falls avoided); the
  wiki holds no common metric to compute a single combined lifetime or DALY effect. This is a structural
  limit (a "needs aggregation" gap), not a queue item.
- **The desirable trajectory is systematically under-measured.** Compressed-morbidity outcomes — the
  *shape* of decline people most value — are counted far less than survival, so a clean mortality result
  can be silent on the outcome that matters most.
- **Per-factor dementia weights are not recoverable** from the held text — the Commission's individual
  communality-adjusted PAFs live in a table not present in the accepted manuscript, so only the 45.3%
  aggregate and the per-factor relative risks are stated here.

## Mandatory caveats

- **The loop is open.** The wiki grades coherence and fidelity to its sources, never validity. No grade
  here has been checked against a realized disease outcome; a clean internal audit is not a validated
  prevention claim.
- **Population fractions are not personal effects.** A "45% attributable" or "12 of 17 cancers linked"
  figure is a population-modelling claim; it does not tell any individual how much of *their* risk a
  change removes.
- **Appraise, do not prescribe.** Screening, dosing, drug choice and titrating any of these against a
  person's labs, medicines and history are prescriber acts the wiki does not perform.
- **These are strata, not a person.** Which outcomes matter, and how length trades against function, is
  elicited case by case and lives outside the graph.
