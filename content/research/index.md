These pages are the guide's working record of how its sources were found and chosen. They are
published **raw**: written for the maintainer, not for a reader, and changed for publication in only
three ways. The search log is split by month, local file paths are removed, and links to internal
pages that are not on this site are shown as plain text.

Expect internal shorthand and tool output (lines such as `acq-engine G contradiction-hunt`). The
[trust page](../trust#how-sources-are-chosen) explains in plain terms how sources are chosen; these
pages are the evidence behind that explanation.

## The search log

Every literature search that fed a source since 31 July 2026: the query, what came back (including
searches that found nothing), and for each candidate whether it was selected, rejected or deferred, and
why. Searches run through the guide's literature tool are logged automatically; web searches are logged
by hand, so a gap is possible.
