<div class="recent-update" data-last-updated="2026-10-04">

This is [[Is the Food Category Doing Any Work]] applied to exercise programming. The food version asks
whether a *category boundary* (meat, dairy, "processed") carries information or averages over a mix that
describes nothing. The exercise version asks the same of a **modality or delivery-format boundary**: when
a decision is posed as *bike vs pool vs land*, *supervised vs home*, *this aerobic mode vs that*, does
the boundary move the patient-important outcome — or, once both options deliver the targeted training
stimulus, is the comparison flat, so the choice is settled by **adherence, feasibility, access, cost, and
injury-avoidance** instead?

The frame already existed for one case: [[Mind-Body Exercise and Cognition]] is, in its own words, this
diagnostic applied to a modality — the apparent Tai-Chi/yoga advantage for cognition dissolves into a
comparator-plus-heterogeneity artifact rather than a mind-body ingredient effect. This page banks the
generalization across the batch of modality/format comparisons the fabric now holds.

</div>

## The pattern — within a goal, the modality axis is mostly flat

Each row is a different modality or delivery choice, tested on its own outcome. The recurring result is
small-to-null between-option differences, with the real lever sitting off the modality axis.

| Choice | Outcome | Between-option result | The lever that remains |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stationary cycling vs other exercise modes | knee-OA symptoms | no mode dominates; no between-mode difference detected | adherence, cost, access, impact-tolerance |
| Aquatic vs land-based | balance / walking in older adults | TUGT and 6MWT show no modality difference; at most a small overall-balance edge for water | feasibility/safety for those who cannot train on land (route-e) |
| Supervised vs unsupervised (home) | function / QoL in >=60 y | only knee-extension strength survives (SMD 0.18, 0.07-0.30); rest lost on sensitivity analysis | delivery format is not load-bearing; attendance was equal (81%) |
| Aerobic vs resistance vs combined | body composition in obesity | no modality reached significance for fat loss | the stimulus (any mode raises CRF), not the mode |
| Mind-body vs aerobic/resistance | cognition | apparent mind-body edge is a comparator artifact | the label does no work beyond generic activity |
| Aerobic vs mind-body vs strengthening vs flexibility/skill vs mixed (NMA) | knee/hip-OA pain, function | single types not shown to differ on pain/function (wide CrIs; point estimates favour aerobic/mind-body; pain trend p = 0.01); **mixed** worse than aerobic and mind-body on pain | preference/adherence among single types; avoid presuming a complex programme is better |
| Endurance vs dynamic resistance vs isometric vs combined (NMA) | SBP (office to ambulatory, mixed) | single types similar; **combined** beats resistance (-2.98, -5.04 to -0.93), and in >=140 mmHg trials beats every single type | baseline SBP (larger effect in hypertensives); combined where feasible, else adherence -> [[Blood Pressure Lowering and Cardiovascular Events]] |

## The decision-change — flip the question

The ordinary framing ("which modality is best?") presumes the boundary carries a ranking. Across these
cases it mostly does not, so the question flips: **pick the option the person will actually sustain and is
least likely to be hurt doing.** This is *adherence-is-part-of-the-effect* and the route-(e)
constraint / route-(c) harm logic made concrete — a sustained lesser-ranked mode beats an abandoned or
injurious "better" one, and when the ranking is flat to begin with there is no benefit being traded away.
It is also *attention-is-an-anti-signal*: the modality debate is loud precisely because the effect
differences are small and contested.

## Two boundaries that keep this honest

**Across goals, modality is NOT flat.** The flatness holds among options that deliver *the same* stimulus.
Different stimuli produce different adaptations: resistance training builds strength and muscle, aerobic
work builds cardiorespiratory fitness, and these do not substitute for each other
-> [[Resistance Training Prescription - Load Sets and Frequency]], [[Measuring and Raising Cardiorespiratory Fitness]].
The claim is *within-goal substitutability*, never *all exercise is interchangeable*.

**Some comparisons do carry a real, graded edge — so check per case, do not assume flat.** The diagnostic
is answered per comparison, exactly as the food version is. [[HIIT vs Continuous Training for Type 2 Diabetes]]
shows HIIT beating MICT on VO2peak at **moderate** certainty (+3.37 ml/kg/min, 1.88-4.87) and on HbA1c at
low certainty (-0.37%), while fasting glucose, insulin and HOMA-IR show no difference
[@liu2019hiit] — an intensity-structure boundary that
*is* informative for fitness and null for glucose, in the same comparison. Combined training for blood
pressure (next paragraph) is a second such case. "Flat" is the modal finding, not a law.

**Blood pressure: single types flat, the combination ahead.** Naci gives each exercise type's SBP effect
against control: «endurance −4.88, 95% CrI −5.69 to −4.06; resistance −3.50, 95% CrI −4.91 to –2.09;
isometric −5.65, 95% CrI −8.21 to −3.13; and combination of endurance and resistance −6.49, 95% CrI −8.17
to −4.82» [@naci2019exercise]. Its type-vs-type
league table finds the single types alike, with one exception: «Overall, different types of structured
exercise interventions achieved similar reductions from baseline (table 2). One excep- tion was the
combination of endurance and resistance training, which was more effective in reducing baseline SBP than
dynamic resistance alone (−2.98, 95% CrI −5.04 to −0.93).»
[@naci2019exercise]. In the sensitivity analysis
restricted to hypertensive trials (>=140 mmHg), the overall exercise effect grows from -4.84 to -8.96, and
the combination beats endurance (-4.81, -7.99 to -1.61), resistance (-6.26, -10.67 to -1.90) and
isometric (-8.58, -14.68 to -2.45) [@naci2019exercise]; against control the combination reaches -13.51 (-16.55 to -10.45), while
isometric's interval includes the null (-4.92, -10.28 to 0.38) [@naci2019exercise]. In the all-populations analysis
the combination's edge over endurance and isometric is not significant; only resistance is beaten. The
authors' text reports these per-type effects against control; the combined-vs-single contrasts in
hypertensives are this page's reading of Table 2.

Three cautions keep the edge provisional. The type contrasts are network estimates, mixing 30 head-to-head
trials with indirect comparison, and the type groups differ in baseline SBP: 134 mmHg endurance, 125
resistance, 129 isometric, 135 combination [@naci2019exercise]. A 10 mmHg baseline gap between resistance and combination trials can produce part of
the all-populations edge on its own, since higher baseline SBP means larger drops. The edge survives the
&gt;=140 mmHg restriction, which partly controls for that. And the combination rests on few trials of
uncertain dose: «For example, only a small subset of studies identified in our review tested the
effectiveness of the combi- nation of endurance and dynamic resistance training. Although these types of
interventions were often effective, especially in hypertensive populations, their frequency and duration
were not consistently reported to determine the optimal formulation and dose of physical activity to
maximise its benefit.» [@naci2019exercise]. So the edge
may reflect more total training rather than the mix itself. The decision is therefore graded, not flat:
among single types, choose on adherence; for a hypertensive person who can do both, the combination is
the option the evidence favours. This is a second graded case beside HIIT.

**Where the boundary is drawn changes the ranking.** The OA network MA is flat among single-type modes,
but its *mixed* bin (more than one core type, or type unspecified) ranks last and is distinguishably
worse than aerobic on pain, SMD 0.64 (0.21 to 1.08) [@gohtypesnma].
An earlier NMA with a different scheme (three types plus their combinations; Uthman, reported via Goh,
not held) found strengthening best for pain and the all-three combination best for function
[@gohtypesnma]. Goh names five
differences between the two NMAs — aim, trial count (103 vs 60), classification, comparator (Uthman
allowed active non-exercise controls) and outcomes — so classification is one candidate cause, not the
proven one. Still, it is an instance of the food-category lesson: a ranking of categories is only as
informative as the category boundary. Why mixed ranks last has three live explanations: a
heterogeneous bin (partly a bin for poorly-described trials) looking like a worse exposure; the
authors' implementation/adherence hypothesis for complex regimens; and small-study inflation of the
thin aerobic and mind-body nodes, the only ones mixed separates from (mixed vs strengthening, the
other large node, 0.26, -0.04 to 0.57). The NMA's lone single-type edge, aerobic over strengthening on
walking-type performance tests, is plausibly training-specificity rather than a modality effect.

## Relation to the food diagnostic — same three-way cut

[[Is the Food Category Doing Any Work]] found the boundary cuts three ways: sometimes it carries the
effect, sometimes it averages over a null mix, sometimes it is predictive yet adds nothing actionable.
Exercise modality behaves the same way: predictive within a goal only when stimulus differs
(across-goal), an averaging-null for most within-goal mode choices, and — mind-body for cognition — a
boundary that looks predictive until the comparator is corrected and then adds nothing. The transferable
move is the diagnostic itself, not a blanket verdict.

## Confidence and the open loop

**Confidence: low.** The constituent findings are themselves low-confidence (small RCT pools, high
heterogeneity, effects that mostly do not survive sensitivity analysis, surrogate outcomes), so the
generalization inherits that ceiling. It is believed as a *decision rule* (choose on adherence/feasibility/
injury when a within-goal modality comparison is flat), not as a proven absence of all modality effects.
No operation here grades the rule against realized adherence or function; it is a statement about the
current comparative evidence, which is systematically thin on exactly the long-horizon, whole-program
outcomes a person cares about.
