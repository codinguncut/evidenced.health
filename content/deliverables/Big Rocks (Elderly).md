# Big Rocks (Elderly)

**A stratum, not a person, and not everyone.** The representative adult here is **around 70, obese with
sarcopenic obesity** (visceral fat *plus* mild muscle loss), commonly **hypertensive, prediabetic/diabetic,
MASLD-leaning**, with **reduced bone density, some anabolic resistance, and increasing polypharmacy** —
the *functionally-drifting* older adult, not the frail or institutionalised (a different stratum). This is
the sibling of [[Big Rocks (Median)]], and the point of the pair is that **several big
rocks INVERT** with age.

## Two shifts reorder everything

1. **The priority OUTCOMES change.** For a 70-year-old, **function, muscle, bone, falls and independence**
   are patient-important endpoints in their own right, and **competing risk shrinks the runway** for pure
   longevity plays. Rank on *staying functional and independent*, not on mortality alone.
2. **The headline lever INVERTS.** For the younger drifting adult the #1 lever is *lose visceral fat*.
   Here, **aggressive energy restriction is a hazard** — it accelerates the sarcopenia and bone loss this
   stratum can least afford. Weight loss becomes *conditional and muscle-protective only*.

*(Caveat throughout: most of the "inverts / harms" reasoning is from mechanism + held age-moderation, not
elderly-specific measured harm — this is conditionality, not evidenced harm.)*

## (a) Robust, function-first — these move to the TOP

1. **Resistance training + adequate protein — the #1 lever here.** Its case for this stratum is the
   strong one: it builds and preserves **muscle, strength and function** and (with activity) reduces
   **falls, injurious falls and probably fractures** in older adults — patient-important outcomes, the
   strongest outcome class the evidence carries for this group ([[Muscle-Strengthening Activity and Mortality]], [[Physical Activity Dose and Mortality]]). **The held protein target is ~1.6 g/kg/day**
   (the dose where protein's added benefit to training-induced lean mass plateaus in healthy adults, wide
   CI ~1.0-2.2; the training is the driver, protein a modest adjunct — [[Protein and Resistance Training for Muscle and Strength]]). Two age caveats that the held source *does* carry: the supplement's added
   lean-mass benefit is **reduced with age**, and adequate protein + RT is **how you keep lean mass during
   any weight loss** — so protein is the guardrail on the weight lever (below). **What the fabric does NOT
   hold is a validated *elderly-specific* target** — whether the anabolic-resistant older adult needs
   *more* than 1.6 g/kg, and the per-meal dose, is the top gap below, not a number to invent here. *(RT's
   independent mortality signal is very-low certainty — not why it earns the top slot; function is.)*
2. **Aerobic activity — for falls, function and mortality.** The falls/fracture reduction above is held
   specifically for older adults; add the general mortality benefit ([[Physical Activity Dose and Mortality]]). *Frailty itself is not targetable* — the evidence base calls it too ill-defined.

## (b) Conditional or INVERTED — the younger levers that flip here

- **Weight / visceral fat — INVERTED.** Do **not** prescribe aggressive energy restriction: it strips
  muscle and bone. If weight loss is pursued (for MASLD, glycaemia, BP, mobility) it must be
  **muscle-and-bone-protective — paired with resistance training and ~1.6 g/kg protein, and gradual**.
  On *rate*: NICE deleted its old specific-deficit number as *arbitrary* and made its aggressive
  low-energy-replacement recommendation deliberately **weak**, citing the harm of *rapid regain / weight
  cycling* ([[Diets for Weight Loss - What NICE Recommends]]) — so "gradual, not rapid" is **guidance-weak
  + mechanism** (faster loss sheds more lean and bone), *not a measured rate threshold*; a measured
  rate/maintenance trial is a routed gap shared with the median cut (Challenge #20). The honest bounds:
  lifestyle weight loss did not cut CV *events* (Look AHEAD null; a 54-RCT
  meta-analysis confirms), though it lowers all-cause mortality by a non-CV route ([[Does Weight Loss Reduce Cardiovascular Events]]); >=5% clears liver fat, 7-10% improves liver inflammation ([[Fatty Liver MASLD and Weight Loss]]) — but for this stratum the sarcopenia/bone cost can outweigh those, so the
  target is *body composition*, not the scale.
- **BP, glycaemia and lipids — age-adjusted, competing-risk-aware, polypharmacy-cautious.** Higher
  *absolute* CV risk means BP-lowering buys real absolute benefit ([[Blood Pressure Lowering and Cardiovascular Events]], intensive control cut events in older high-risk adults in SPRINT — but with
  more hypotension/syncope/AKI, [[Baseline Risk and the Relative-Absolute Split]]). Lipids: LDL/apoB
  *causes* ASCVD and the dose is *cumulative* ([[LDL ApoB and Cumulative Exposure]]) — but **competing
  risk and a short runway blunt the lifetime payoff**, which is why guidance pulls back with age (ESC
  drops lipid-lowering to Class **IIb at >=70**, USPSTF finds statin *initiation* insufficient at **>=76** —
  [[Statins for Primary Prevention and the Power of Zero CAC]], [[SCORE2 Baseline Risk and the ESC Treatment Thresholds]], [[Lifetime Benefit - The Frame for Younger Adults]]). **Polypharmacy + fall /
  orthostatic risk make over-treatment its own harm** — so *appraise, do not prescribe* binds harder here
  than anywhere.

## (c) Small / contested — further demoted below function

- **SFA -> unsaturated**, **free sugars**, **red/processed meat** (the contested tension — and note meat
  is a *protein source that matters more here*): small levers, demoted below the muscle/function priority
  ([[Saturated Fat Intake and Replacement]], [[Free Sugars Intake]], [[Should Adults Reduce Red and Processed Meat]]).
- **Sodium — with a reversal-of-emphasis:** a small BP lever, but **over-restriction (of sodium or energy)
  can worsen appetite, intake and frailty in the old** — so the aggressive-cut framing of the younger
  guide is itself a hazard here ([[Sodium Intake and Blood Pressure]]).
- **Alcohol** (interacts with polypharmacy and the liver) and **sleep** (7-8 h; the long-sleep arm a
  marker not a target): unchanged levers ([[Alcohol and Mortality and Vascular Disease]], [[Sleep Duration and Mortality]]).

## The gaps — this cut's main product (routed for acquisition)

The fabric is **thin on the elderly-specific questions**, and naming them is the deliverable's real value:

- **Sarcopenia / anabolic-resistance protein dosing** — the mainstream mechanism (anabolic resistance ->
  a *higher per-meal leucine threshold* to trigger muscle protein synthesis -> a case for higher, more
  *pulsed*, higher-leucine/higher-quality protein in the old; Challenge #22) is **plausible but UNHELD**,
  and it sits in *apparent tension* with the only datum the fabric holds — Morton found the supplement's
  added lean-mass benefit *reduced* with age, not enhanced. Resolving that tension (does reduced response
  mean *give up on supplementing* or *the old need more to overcome resistance*?) is what a dedicated
  PROT-AGE / per-meal-dose source would settle. **Top acquirable gap** -> `ingest-queue.md`.
- **Bone mineral density** interventions (loading exercise, protein, weight) — essentially unheld.
- **Intentional weight loss WITH muscle/bone preservation in the elderly** — the central trade-off, no
  elderly-specific trial held.
- **Rate of loss and the maintenance phase** — no held trial on *how fast* to lose (the "gradual, not
  rapid" above is mechanism, not a measured threshold) or on the post-loss maintenance phase; a shared
  gap with [[Big Rocks (Median)]] (Challenge #20) -> `ingest-queue.md`.
- **The obesity paradox in the old** — whether moderate overweight is protective via reserve/competing
  risk — unheld, and it bears directly on the weight lever.
- **Grip strength / muscle mass as outcome predictors** — staged (Leong/PURE), not yet ingested; a strong
  predictor for this stratum (Challenge #17).
- **Deprescribing / polypharmacy** — unheld and largely **out of scope** (a prescriber act) — named, not
  appraised.
- **Elderly-specific CV/BP/statin trials** (SPRINT-elderly subgroup, HYVET, PROSPER; SCORE2-OP staged) —
  would firm the age-adjusted section.

## The honest limits

- **Open loop:** coherence and source-fidelity, never validity.
- **Appraise, do not prescribe — and it binds HARDER here:** polypharmacy, deprescribing, drug
  interactions, fall-risk titration and BP/glucose target-setting in the old are prescriber acts this
  cannot do.
- **A stratum, not a person, and not everyone:** the frail/institutionalised elderly are a *different*
  stratum, and the fit older adult differs again; the inversions above are the *default*, tuned per case.
- **Gap-heavy by construction:** the elderly-specific evidence is largely unheld, so much of the "inverts"
  reasoning is mechanism + age-moderation, not measured elderly harm — stated as conditionality.
