title: "Search log: how it is kept"
---
The going-forward record of how sources were **found** — the reproducible-search half of the
trustworthiness initiative (trustworthiness #R9, Tier 2). One entry per literature search that
feeds acquisition. Append-only; grep `^## \[` for the index.

## Required contents — the four-field policy (binding, every lit-search) `[maintainer 2026-08-05]`

Every literature-research / paper-search episode — via `bin/openalex.py`, web search, or any other
channel — records ALL FOUR fields here (mirror in CLAUDE.md -> *Bias machinery* -> SEARCH-LEDGER POLICY):

1. **Query** — the terms, the channel (openalex / web / other), and the filters/date used.
2. **Results** — the hits (DOIs/titles), AND the **zero-hit queries** — a 0 is data, and it is attributed
   (genuine literature absence vs a tool artifact, e.g. `title.search` requiring every word in the title).
   A silent 0 read as "no evidence exists" is the exact failure this ledger prevents.
3. **Adjudication + reason** — per candidate **SELECTED / REJECTED / DEFERRED**, each with the *why*
   (newer-supersedes, OA-vs-paywalled, design tier, too-new/uncited, off-question). The **negative space —
   what was rejected and why — is the load-bearing content**, not a courtesy; asymmetric scrutiny hides
   here, so it must be visible.
4. **Acquisition-requested** — which candidates were sent to acquire, and their **fetch status**
   (fetched to `inbox/` vs blocked -> maintainer-drop). Never downgrade to a weaker source on a fetch fail
   — pause and ask (the acquisition-pause-and-ask rule).

**Mechanical vs judgment split:** `openalex.py` **auto-logs fields (1)+(2)** (the raw query line). A
**web/other-channel search has NO auto-log — log it by hand.** Fields (3)+(4) are `[judgment-only]`: write
a curated `## [YYYY-MM-DD] SELECTION RECORD — <topic>` block every session; the raw query lines never
stand alone. The auto-log proves *what ran*; the SELECTION RECORD proves *how it was judged*.

**Acquisition-ENGINE runs (A/B/C/D/E) are tracked the same way, in three tiers.** The five `acq_*.py`
engines call `oa._get()` directly, so they bypass the per-query `_log`; instead each ends its run with
`oa.log_engine_run(...)` (default on; `--no-log` to skip), appending ONE `- [date] acq-engine <X> [params]
-> <counts> | top: <dois> pool-tsv=<path>` line. That is fields (1)+(2) for an engine: the params, the
anti-join-resolved pool counts (candidates / held-dropped / rejected-source soft-matches), and the top
shortlist. **The FULL candidate pool lives in the byte-diffable `--write` tsv** (`acq-<engine>.tsv`), which
the line names — that is where "all candidates" persist durably. **Promotions/rejections are the same
`SELECTION RECORD` block** (fields 3+4): a candidate from the pool becomes PROMOTED (acquired) / DEFERRED /
REJECTED there, adjudicated against the mechanical line. So: engine line = what ran; tsv = the full pool;
SELECTION RECORD = the per-candidate verdict.

**Scope + honesty (read before trusting this log):**

- **Going-forward only — but the back-catalogue is PARTIALLY recovered.** This log starts 2026-07-31;
  searches before it were not captured here (source selection grew breadth-first — the trust page's
  historical-looseness caveat). Those past searches are *partially* recovered by mining the `.claude`
  session transcripts: **`search-log-recovered.md`** (generated by `bin/recover_search_provenance.py`)
  links **40/129** held sources to the acquisition search that named them (07-25..07-31). It does not
  pretend to a clean retroactive trail — 23 links are DOI-confident, the rest are name-candidates, and
  \~89 sources are unrecoverable (dropped via `inbox/`, pre-transcript, or unnamed). Heal what's
  healable; disclose the residual.
- **ChatGPT-sourced research is recovered too.** Some sources came from research the maintainer ran in
  ChatGPT (a question posed, a candidate reading list returned), then assessed + DOI-checked here.
  That trail is captured in **`search-log-chatgpt.md`** (from `bin/parse_chatgpt_research.py` over the
  archived transcript in `provenance/`): 4 research questions, 68 candidates, **10 held**, 58 surfaced
  but not taken — an honest identification -> screening record, including the candidates rejected.
- **The STAGED ingest queue is provenance-retrofitted too.** `search-log-stage-provenance` isn't
  where it lives — see **`stage-provenance.md`** (from `bin/stage_provenance.py`): all **156 staged
  (un-ingested) papers** attributed across the three channels — **Claude web searches 108 (+11 both),
  ChatGPT 20, reading-list 16** (method/books), 12 matcher-miss. Confirms most sources came from Claude
  searches, a chunk from ChatGPT, the method base from the reading list — almost none invented.
- **The EXCLUSIONS are tracked with reasons.** `rejected-sources.md` — candidates assessed and NOT
  acquired, each with a brief reason (surrogate / GLP-1-saturated / superseded / redundant / peripheral
  / advisory-not-SR / off-scope), recovered from the actual 2026-07-30 acquire-reject decisions.
- **The Claude-side decision REASONING is recovered too — at two grains.** (1) The one 2026-07-30
  *keystone sweep*: `keystone-acquisition-review.md`, the Claude analog of `search-log-chatgpt.md` —
  7-domain research sweep, 4 ranked gaps, a Tier-A/B catalog where **every candidate carries a
  design/tier + a "fills/unblocks" reason**. (2) The scattered *day-to-day* searches embedded in
  ingest/weave/query flows across the 6 days: `search-log-reasoning.md` (from `bin/search_reasoning.py`)
  — the assistant text that FOLLOWED each search (what was found + why taken/skipped: "WGO gold-standard
  guideline, freely available", "De-Regil Cochrane folate->NTD RR 0.28", "umbrella review OA but
  JS-gated"). Operational-grade, honestly labeled; \~58 of \~150 carried reasoning, 11 substantive after
  filtering mechanics.

- **Per-source ingest rationale is recovered — from the curated log, not mined windows.**
  `source-reasoning.md` (from `bin/source_reasoning.py`): for every held domain source, its real "why"
  from **`log.md`** (the authoritative one-entry-per-ingest record — "domain-opener", "pairing with
  GRADE", "the second guidance family arrives", "KEEP; predictions 4/4"), with a keystone-review
  fallback. **81/99**; the founding 07-25..28 set is fully covered (they were never a gap — the ingest
  log recorded their rationale at ingest time). The remaining \~18 are recent cluster-logged batches, a
  naming-match limit not a gap. NB: mining transcript *windows* for per-source rationale was tried and
  rejected — it surfaced usage-mentions, not decisions (would have dressed a nearby mention up as the
  reason); the curated log is the honest source.

**The two research channels are symmetric, and the trail is complete:** searches
(`search-log-recovered` = Claude · `search-log-chatgpt` = ChatGPT), reasoning
(`keystone-acquisition-review` = Claude · `search-log-chatgpt` + triage = ChatGPT), inclusions
(`stage-provenance`), exclusions (`rejected-sources`). The identification -> screening -> selection
trail the initiative wants, kept light and honest.
- **Not a systematic-review search.** These are *targeted* searches under the acquisition rule
  (reviews / meta-analyses / umbrella reviews / guideline bodies first), not exhaustive PRISMA
  searches. A log entry documents what was done, not a claim of completeness.
- **This is a discipline, not a gate `[judgment-only]`.** Appended during `acquire` / `plan queue` /
  `process inbox`. Lightweight by design — the point is a truthful trail, not bureaucracy.

## Entry format

```
## [YYYY-MM-DD] <topic / decision question being sourced>
- Searched: <databases / bodies> — <terms or strategy>
- Filters: <date / language / study-design limits, or "none">
- Found / screened: <n> / <n>
- Included: <registry slug(s) staged or acquired>
- Excluded (notable): <source> — <reason>
- Notes: <keystone found? none found -> named gap? tier of what was taken?>
```

- **Included** names the canonical registry slug so an entry reconciles with `sources.md` and the
  `via` selection-provenance axis (a search-sourced entry is `via = pick` unless it came from a
  keystone bibliography — see trustworthiness §2).
- **Notes** should say when a search returned *no* keystone-tier source for a live sub-topic — that is
  a coverage gap (type-G), a finding in its own right, not a licence to grab a weaker primary study.
