**Nucleus of the `glp1-reward` cluster (three gold sources: substance craving, depression, suicidality).**
GLP-1-receptor agonists (semaglutide, liraglutide, exenatide/exendin-4, dulaglutide) reduce food craving
by acting on mesolimbic reward circuitry. This page answers the question that raises: does that reward
effect **generalize beyond food** — and if it does, is the direction **benefit** (less pathological
craving) or **harm** (a general blunting of pleasure and motivation, i.e. anhedonia)? The opening source
is a narrative systematic review of GLP-1RAs across substance use disorders (SUDs) — alcohol, nicotine,
cocaine, opioids [@volker2026glp1sud]; two human-mood SR+MAs
now sit beside it — Bi 2026 on depression risk and Bushi 2025 on suicidality — covering the harm side of
the same mechanism. The node is opened and partly filled; it is not saturated (no human anhedonia/QoL
outcome, no pooled RCT-AE suicidality arm).

**The headline: cross-domain reward reduction is real, and the evidenced valence inverts the anhedonia
premise.** The same mesolimbic-dopamine mechanism that reduces food craving also reduces *substance*
craving, and where it has been measured the effect is **predominantly beneficial** (less alcohol, less
nicotine consumption) and — in animal models — **substance-selective**, sparing natural-food, water and
saccharin reward. That cuts against a *flattens all pleasure* reading. The crucial honest limit: **no
human study here measured hedonic tone, anhedonia, or quality of life** — the selectivity evidence is
animal-only, so the anhedonia harm is *unmeasured, not refuted*.
[inferred from @volker2026glp1sud]

**Update (2026-09-15) — the human MOOD arm has partially landed, and it leans HARM.** A second gold
source (Bi 2026, SR+MA) supplies the human mood-outcome data the paragraph above called absent: pooled
**OR 1.49 (95% CI 1.18-1.88)** for depression-related adverse events among GLP-1 RA users
[@glp1depression2026]. This does **not** overturn the benefit-leaning
reading of the *substance-craving* arm (a different outcome), and it does **not** measure anhedonia /
hedonic tone / QoL directly (its outcome is depression-related AEs, adjacent but not identical) — so the
specific anhedonia gap stays open. What it changes: the *feared mood harm is no longer unmeasured*.
Human evidence now exists on **both** arms of the one mechanism — benefit on substance craving (Volker),
harm on depression AEs (Bi) — which is exactly the **bidirectional, susceptibility-dependent** valence
the mechanism predicts (see the Bi section below). The honest composite: a selective benefit where the
pathological reinforcer is strong, a mood-harm signal in susceptible individuals, and the pleasure-tone
outcome still unmeasured. [inferred from @volker2026glp1sud; @glp1depression2026]

## Scope placement — why this is in scope, and where it stops

GLP-1RAs are a **standard drug for a major lifestyle stratum** (obesity), so what their reward mechanism
does *beyond* appetite is a pharmacotherapy-taper limitation question — in scope as a drug
efficacy/limitation appraisal. The **anhedonia** angle (does the drug cost a person their capacity for
joy?) is a patient-important-outcome question on the drug most of the target population may take. What is
**frontier / prescriber-zone** and deliberately NOT built here: SUD *treatment* selection, dosing, or
titration — the specific treatment of a diagnosed disease. The SUD evidence enters as **evidence for the
reward-valence question**, not as an addiction-treatment guide.

## The mechanism generalizes — same reward circuitry as food craving `[type-F / cross-domain]`

The preclinical mechanism is consistent across substances: **attenuation of substance-induced dopamine
release in the nucleus accumbens (NAc)**, with GLP-1 receptors in the VTA, NAc, nucleus tractus
solitarius (NTS), lateral septum and laterodorsal tegmentum implicated; nicotine adds the NTS -> medial
habenula-interpeduncular circuit, opioids add GLP-1/orexin interactions
[@volker2026glp1sud]. This is the **same mesolimbic
pathway** GLP-1 engages to dampen food reward — so the drug's appetite effect and its substance-craving
effect are two expressions of one reward-circuit action, not separate properties. That is the mechanistic
basis for taking the cross-domain (and the anhedonia) question seriously in the first place.
[inferred from @volker2026glp1sud]

## The selectivity finding — the anti-anhedonia crux `[animal-only, directional]`

Across animal models the reward reduction was **targeted at the pathological reinforcer, not a blanket
motivational deficit** — natural reward was repeatedly spared:

- Ethanol self-administration fell up to 70% with exendin-4, yet «This reduction was not accompanied by a
  decrease in operant responses for a palatable liquid food reward, demonstrating that Exendin-4's
  effects were speciﬁc to alcohol and not related to general motivational deﬁcits or metabolic factors.»
  [@volker2026glp1sud]
- For heroin, «liraglutide's effects were speciﬁc to drug-related behaviors, as it did not alter natural
  reward-seeking or saccharin intake.» [@volker2026glp1sud]
- Cocaine work showed the same behavioral specificity (sucrose-seeking, food intake and body weight
  spared) and, on a D1-agonist control, action on cocaine reinforcement «not on general dopaminergic
  tone» [@volker2026glp1sud].

**What this licenses and what it does not.** It is *directional* evidence that GLP-1 reward modulation is
selective rather than global — the mechanism does not flatten all reward in a rat. It does **not** settle
the human anhedonia question: these are animal operant paradigms, not measures of subjective pleasure or
meaning, and the transportability caveat binds (animal != human). The correct state is
**insufficient-evidence** on human anhedonia, with the animal signal pointing away from the harm rather
than confirming it. [inferred from @volker2026glp1sud]

## Per-substance direction and certainty

The evidence is lopsided (alcohol 24 studies, cocaine 8, nicotine 5, opioids fewest) and mostly
preclinical (only six of 42 studies are human)
[@volker2026glp1sud].

- **Alcohol — benefit, strongest arm.** Three human datasets point the same way with caveats: exenatide
  cut alcohol cue-reactivity and helped the BMI>30 subgroup but not heavy-drinking-days overall (Klausen
  2022); semaglutide reduced lab self-administration (Hendershot 2025); dulaglutide gave a «29% reduction
  in weekly alcohol intake, which increased to 36% when adjusted for education level ... though the
  heaviest drinkers did not exhibit signiﬁcant reductions» (Probst 2023)
  [@volker2026glp1sud]. Preclinical support is substantial
  and includes a nonhuman-primate study.
- **Nicotine — benefit.** Reduced daily cigarettes (CO-verified, semaglutide), improved abstinence and
  reduced withdrawal (exenatide+NRT), and reduced post-cessation weight gain (dulaglutide)
  [@volker2026glp1sud].
- **Cocaine — NULL in humans, benefit preclinical.** The one human trial found «No signiﬁcant differences
  ... regarding the number of cocaine infusions, self-reported euphoria, or craving» (single-dose, small
  n) [@volker2026glp1sud], while rodent work showed reduced
  reinstatement. A clean divergence between the animal and the (thin) human evidence.
- **Opioid — mixed / uncertain, no human data.** Liraglutide suppressed heroin/fentanyl seeking
  preclinically (efficacy «paralleled that of buprenorphine ... without acting as an opioid agonist
  itself»), BUT exendin-4 «did not attenuate morphine-induced conditioned place preference ... In some
  instances, Ex4 even increased the reinforcing effects of remifentanil»
  [@volker2026glp1sud]. Agent-specific and unresolved.

## Effect modification — a route-(b) signal, held as preliminary

Two modifiers recur, both consistent with the alcohol clinical data:

- **Higher baseline BMI -> larger effect** (alcohol): «trials investigating exenatide and semaglutide in
  alcohol use disorder reported that participants with higher baseline body mass index showed more
  pronounced treatment effects» [@volker2026glp1sud].
- **Higher baseline consumption -> larger effect**, with GLP-1R expression in the NAc shell elevated in
  and correlated with heavy intake (preclinical); and **sex differences** (males more persistent after
  discontinuation) [@volker2026glp1sud].

These are **positive interaction signals (route b — the false-positive-prone route)** on a thin,
mostly-animal base. Held as candidate effect-modifiers, not established ones — the honest state is a
plausible dose-of-pathology gradient (the drug does more where the pathological reward is stronger),
awaiting adequately powered human interaction tests.
[inferred from @volker2026glp1sud]

## Real-world signal on a hard outcome

Flagged by the SR but outside its inclusion criteria: «a registry-based cohort including more than
200,000 individuals with AUD reported reduced alcohol-related health events among GLP-1RA users»
(Lähteenvuo 2025) [@volker2026glp1sud]. This is the one
pointer to a **patient-important** endpoint (health events, not craving), but it is unappraised here. — a held appraisal of this cohort would move
the alcohol arm from surrogate/consumption toward a hard outcome.

## The human mood arm — a harm-leaning depression signal [@glp1depression2026]

Bi 2026 is a systematic review and meta-analysis (six databases, RCTs + observational, adults with
overweight/obesity, mostly T2D) of GLP-1 RA depression risk — the first pooled *human* mood outcome on
this node. It leans the opposite way to the substance-craving arm:

- **Pooled OR 1.49 (95% CI 1.18-1.88)** for depression-related adverse events (treatment-emergent
  depression, worsening symptoms, mood fluctuations, suicide-related), «indicating a 49% relative increase
  in the risk of depression-related outcomes among GLP-1 RA users compared with controls»
  [@glp1depression2026].
- **Direction stable, magnitude not.** «15 of 18 individual point estimates lay on the "increased-risk"
  side of the null», significant under both DerSimonian-Laird and REML
  [@glp1depression2026]; robust across leave-one-out, and no publication
  bias (Egger P = 0.952) [@glp1depression2026]. BUT heterogeneity is
  extreme («Cochran's Q = 886.46, df = 7, p < 0.001; I² = 99.2%, τ² = 0.11»)
  [@glp1depression2026], so the pooled number averages a very
  heterogeneous set — direction more secure than magnitude. GRADE = **moderate** for depression (low /
  very low for suicidality).

**Two limits temper the harm reading, neither refutes it.** The outcome is a mix of clinically-diagnosed
depression, passive adverse-event capture, and self-report scales — «some reporting clinically diagnosed
depression or depression-related adverse events, while others used self-reported symptom scales»
[@glp1depression2026] — heterogeneous and part-[[Surrogate Outcomes|surrogate]].
And **confounding by indication** is the load-bearing threat: the exposed population (obese, T2D,
psychiatric-comorbid) carries an elevated *baseline* depression risk, since «obese individuals, patients
with type 2 diabetes, and subgroups with mental disorders, have significantly increased baseline
depression and suicide risks themselves» [@glp1depression2026]. (An
internal study-count inconsistency — 19 studies stated vs df = 7 vs 18 estimates — is a further
data-quality caveat, detailed on the source page.)

### The bidirectional mechanism unifies both arms — coherence, NOT a tension `[type-F]`

Bi's own mechanism account is the same mesolimbic-dopamine circuit this page's mechanism section builds
on, read from the harm side. GLP-1 activation «may reduce the response of the midbrain dopamine system to
pleasurable stimuli and decrease reward-driven behaviors» — which «can explain the potential benefits of
GLP-1RA in treating anorexia and behavioral addiction» (the Volker benefit arm) — but «the same neural
regulation may manifest in some individuals as symptoms such as anhedonia, reduced motivation and low
mood, which are precisely the important core manifestations of depressive disorders»
[@glp1depression2026]. Bi flags the state of it: «This bidirectional
possibility has shown signs in basic research, but has not yet been sys­ tematically verified in human
studies» [@glp1depression2026].

So the benefit-leaning substance arm and the harm-leaning depression arm are **not a joined tension** to
file — the not-joined check (ii) fires (different outcomes: substance craving vs mood/depression), and
both are *predicted arms of one susceptibility-dependent mechanism*. This is a **type-F refinement**: the
composite (animal-selective benefit on pathological reward + human depression-AE harm in susceptible
individuals) is a claim neither source states alone, and it beats either alone. It is **NOT type-E** —
Bi and Volker measure different outcomes, and Bi's trial base overlaps the wiki's canonical GLP-1 RCTs
(STEP, LEADER, SCALE), so any GLP-1-corpus agreement is shared-trial-base, not independent backing.
[inferred from @volker2026glp1sud; @glp1depression2026]

## The severe endpoint — suicidality shows no population signal, but watch the psychiatric-comorbid stratum [@glp1suicidality2025]

Bushi 2025 is the dedicated SR+MA of the one outcome that turned this from an academic mood question
into a **regulatory event**: suicidal ideation and behaviour. GLP-1RA neuropsychiatric worry
«prompt[ed] investigations by regulatory bodies such as the FDA and EMA» in 2023, and this review
is the pooled answer to that specific scare [@glp1suicidality2025].
Its base is **observational + pharmacovigilance** (11 studies, one post hoc trial analysis; NO
pooled RCT-AE arm), so it is a different, weaker design than the CV trials — GRADE for suicidality
is low/very-low (as [[Depression and Modifiable Exposures]] and Bi 2026 also note).

- **Pooled: no signal, but the estimate is nearly uninformative.** «The overall RR using a
  random-effects model was 0.568 (95% CI: 0.077-4.205), indicating no statistically significant
  difference in the risk of suicidal outcomes between GLP-1RAs and other drug classes. However,
  substantial heterogeneity was observed (I2 = 98%, p < 0.01)» [@glp1suicidality2025], pooling only 4 of 11 studies. The point estimate leans *protective* but
  the CI runs from strong protection to 4x harm, and «The wide prediction interval (0.001- 218.938)
  further suggests considerable uncertainty in the estimated effect» [@glp1suicidality2025]. This is *no statistically significant association*, not a tight
  reassuring null — read it as *no population signal amid large uncertainty*, not *proven safe*.
- **The narrative synthesis leans reassuring-to-protective.** Pharmacovigilance studies
  «consistently reported no disproportionate increase in suicidality» (Chen ROR 0.16, Kim ROR 0.15)
  and cohorts leaned protective — Wang 2024 (semaglutide, obese) «reported a lower risk for suicidal
  ideation and self-injury compared with non-GLP-1RAs, with hazard ratios of 0.27 and 0.44»
  [@glp1suicidality2025]; Nassar OR 0.461 vs DPP-4; Hurtado
  null (HR 1.04, 1.36). Sample sizes ran to >690,000 users.
- **The counter-signal is agent- and stratum-specific.** A WHO-VigiBase disproportionality analysis
  «found a significant signal for semaglutide-related suicidal ideation, with a reporting odds ratio
  (ROR) of 1.45 (95% CI: 1.18-1.77). Notably, the risk was substantially higher in patients who were
  co-prescribed antidepressants (ROR: 4.45, 95% CI: 2.52-7.86) or benzodiazepines (ROR: 4.07, 95%
  CI: 1.69-9.82)» [@glp1suicidality2025]. Read with the
  route-(b) caveat: co-prescription of antidepressants/benzodiazepines is a **marker of pre-existing
  psychiatric illness**, so the elevated ROR is heavily **confounded by indication**, not clean
  effect modification — but it points at the same *susceptible stratum* Bi's depression signal does.
- **A different-design check agrees.** Bushi cites a Mendelian-randomization study (Nguyen 2024)
  that «found no causal evidence linking the glycaemic and BMI-lowering effects of GLP-1RAs to an
  increased risk of suicide attempts, either in the general population or in individuals with
  pre-existing mental health conditions» [@glp1suicidality2025].
  A genetic natural experiment pointing the same reassuring way as the pooled observational estimate
  strengthens the null direction — but it is cited *within* Bushi, so it is within-source
  triangulation, not the wiki's independent-E.

### How suicidality relates to the depression arm — REFINE, not contradiction `[type-F]`

The reassuring suicidality picture does **not** overturn Bi's harm-leaning depression signal (OR
1.49): the not-joined check (ii) fires — **different outcomes** (completed/attempted suicide and
suicidal ideation vs depression-related adverse events), and different severity tiers of the same
mood axis. The honest composite across the two sources is a **graded severity signal**: a
harm-leaning signal on the milder, higher-frequency endpoint (depression AEs, Bi) and **no robust
population signal on the severe endpoint** (suicidality, Bushi) — with **both** converging on the
same stratum to watch (psychiatric-comorbid / already on psychotropics). That convergence is
coherence via the susceptibility-dependent mechanism, not independent backing.

**This is NOT type-E.** Bushi and Bi measure different outcomes; Bushi shares the FAERS/WHO-VigiBase
pharmacovigilance substrate and the trial-derived data (O'Neil = SCALE-program liraglutide) with any
GLP-1 corpus, so even the adjacent agreement is shared-base. No `[E-independent]` claimed.
[inferred from @glp1depression2026; @glp1suicidality2025]

## Evidence state and decision relevance

- **Cross-domain reward reduction: benefit-leaning, low certainty.** Direction is consistent for alcohol
  and nicotine (human + animal); null for cocaine in humans; unresolved for opioids. No pooled magnitude
  exists — narrative synthesis of heterogeneous designs, `G (needs aggregation)`.
- **Mood / depression: harm-leaning human signal (OR 1.49), low-to-moderate certainty.** Bi's pooled
  human depression-AE signal now points *toward* a mood harm — tempering, without overturning, the
  animal-selectivity optimism. Direction is stable; magnitude sits on I²=99% and confounding by
  indication, so it is a real but low-quality signal (GRADE moderate).
- **Suicidality (the severe endpoint): no meaningful population signal, on a weak base.** Bushi's pooled
  RR 0.568 (CI 0.077-4.205) shows no statistically significant association, the narrative synthesis and a
  Mendelian-randomization check lean reassuring, and the only counter-signal is a confounded,
  semaglutide-specific ROR in the psychiatric-comorbid stratum. Read as *no population signal amid large
  uncertainty*, GRADE low/very-low — not "proven safe." Coexists with (does not contradict) the
  depression-AE harm signal, the two being a graded severity pair on one mood axis.
- **Anhedonia / hedonic tone / QoL specifically: still insufficient-evidence.** The *GLP-1 stole my joy*
  narrative is neither confirmed nor refuted: the reward blunting looks selective in animals, and Bi
  measures depression-related *adverse events*, not subjective pleasure/meaning. So the specific
  hedonic-tone outcome remains unmeasured even though an adjacent mood-harm signal has landed. Report the
  narrowed gap, do not close it.
- **Decision-change this licenses.** For someone weighing a GLP-1RA and worried it will flatten all
  enjoyment: the best current evidence says the reward effect is (in animals) targeted at pathological
  reinforcers and (in humans) shows up as reduced *substance* craving, a benefit — with human hedonic-tone
  data still absent. That reframes a feared harm as an **unmeasured question with a benefit-leaning
  prior**, which is itself a decision-relevant update over silence.

[inferred from @volker2026glp1sud]

## Gaps and open questions

- **G — human mood arm PARTIALLY landed (Bi 2026, OR 1.49 harm); anhedonia / QoL / hedonic-tone still
  open.** The broad mood-outcome gap is now partially cashed by Bi's depression-AE MA (see the human-mood
  section) — the feared harm is no longer unmeasured, and it leans harm. But the *specific* hedonic-tone /
  anhedonia / quality-of-life outcome the obesity stratum most cares about is still unmeasured: a
  depression-AE count is not a pleasure-tone measure. Narrowed gap — a GLP-1 -> anhedonia/QoL analysis
  with a prospective hedonic-tone instrument would close it. (Adjacent held fabric on GLP-1
  neuropsychiatric safety: [[GLP-1 Non-Cardiometabolic Effects and Safety]]; the depression-lever context:
  [[Depression and Modifiable Exposures]].)
- **G — suicidality sub-outcome now cashed (Bushi 2025), but the RCT-AE arm and the psychiatric-comorbid
  stratum remain open.** The severe endpoint is landed as a population null on a weak observational/
  pharmacovigilance base (see the severe-endpoint section). Two holes stay: (i) no pooled *RCT-AE*
  suicidality estimate exists — the meta-analysis was observational + spontaneous-report, and trials
  systematically **exclude** people with psychiatric history, so the population that matters is the one the
  clean design cannot see; (ii) the semaglutide-specific signal in psychotropic-co-prescribed users is
  confounded by indication and unresolved — a design that separates drug effect from baseline risk in the
  psychiatric-comorbid stratum would close it. — a suicidality analysis restricted to
  RCT-adjudicated events, or one that de-confounds the comorbid stratum.
- **G — opioids and stimulants understudied in humans** (the SR's own priority): «the clinical evidence
  base is underdeveloped and highly unbalanced»
  [@volker2026glp1sud].
- **G — dose-response for reward/addiction unstudied**; SUD doses assumed equal to metabolic doses.
- **Not yet a joined tension with food-reward reduction.** Whether the substance-craving benefit and the
  appetite effect are the *same* selective mechanism or separable is open — the mechanism section reads
  them as one, but no source tests them against each other. Related construct, different object:
  [[Food Addiction]] is food-as-addiction (the YFAS reward construct); this page is the *drug's* action on
  non-food reward. `[type-B]`
